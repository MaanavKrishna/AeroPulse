Why it exists

Booking sites tell you the price of one search. Nobody publishes an open, long-running record of how fares move across the country, so every festival season the same question gets answered with press releases. AeroPulse collects fares the same way every day, publishes the method, and lets anyone download the numbers.

The site sets no tracking cookies and loads nothing from third parties. Your recent route choices are remembered only in your own browser.

Demo data

When the site runs without a provider key it fills itself with synthetic fares, so it can be developed and tested. Those fares are generated, never real, and every page says so in a banner while any of them are present. The public site never mixes demo data with real data, and the software refuses to start in production with the demo generator switched on.
